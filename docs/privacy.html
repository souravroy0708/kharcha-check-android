Kharcha Check privacy policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Short version Your money data stays on your phone. There is no account and no server holding your entries. The internet is used only to download public mutual fund prices, and backups go only where you choose.

What we collect

Nothing automatically. Kharcha Check has no account and no server that stores your entries. There are no analytics, advertising or tracking tools in the app.

How the app uses the internet

Mutual fund prices: the app downloads the public daily NAV file published by AMFI (amfiindia.com), which lists every fund. Because the whole list is downloaded and searched on your phone, the request reveals nothing about which funds you hold. No personal data is sent. All connections use HTTPS.

Automatic backup (optional): if you turn it on, the app writes a password-encrypted backup file to a folder you choose. If you choose a cloud folder such as Google Drive, that provider's app uploads the already-encrypted file; we never receive it and cannot read it.

Crash reports (optional)

If the app crashes, technical details (app version, Android version, phone model and the error trace) are saved on your phone. Next time you open the app, you can choose to email them to the developer; you see the email before it is sent. Reports contain no amounts, payees or notes. If you send one, it is used only to fix the problem.

What stays on your phone

The entries, categories, budgets, goals and settings you create. They are stored in an encrypted database (AES-256) whose key is protected by your phone's secure hardware. Android's automatic cloud backup is turned off for this app.

SMS

Kharcha Check does not request SMS permission and never reads your messages. If you choose to copy a bank message and paste or share it into the app, only that text is read, on your phone.

Photos, screenshots and statements

If you attach a profile photo, a receipt, a payment screenshot or a bank, credit card or UPI statement, the app copies it into its own private storage and encrypts it with a key held by your phone's secure hardware. Files are picked with Android's photo or file picker, so the app never gets access to your whole gallery or storage. Attachments are never uploaded by the app. If "Include statements & receipts in backups" is on (the default), they go inside your password-encrypted backup file. If you choose "Open in app", a temporary decrypted copy is passed to the app you pick and deleted the next time Kharcha Check starts.

Automatic reading (optional)

If "Auto-read screenshots & statements" is on, the app reads text from the screenshots and statements you choose using open-source engines (PdfBox for digital PDFs, Tesseract for images and scans) that run entirely on your phone. No image or text is sent anywhere. A statement's PDF password, if you enter one, is used once to open the file and is never stored. Detected transactions are only suggestions; you review them before anything is saved. You can turn this off in Settings.

Backups and exports

Backups (.kcbak) are encrypted with a password you choose and saved only where you choose. PDF and CSV exports are created on your phone and saved where you choose. We never receive them.

Notifications

Optional daily reminders and month-end report notifications are generated on your phone. Amounts are hidden on the lock screen.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Delete everything, or uninstall the app. Because nothing is stored elsewhere, that removes it completely.

Children

Kharcha Check is intended for adults (18+).
