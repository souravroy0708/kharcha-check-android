Plan

Private by design

Your money is nobody’s business. Not even ours.

Most money apps ask to read every SMS on your phone. Kharcha Check never does. There is no account and no server holding your entries, so there is nothing of yours for anyone to sell, leak or hand over.

  • Encrypted on your phoneAES-256 database with its key locked in your phone’s secure hardware. Receipts and statements are encrypted too.
  • Nothing uploadedStatements and screenshots are read on the phone, offline. Backups go only to a folder you pick, locked with your password.
  • Checked on every buildThe build fails automatically if an SMS, contacts, location or advertising permission ever sneaks in.

Every permission, in full

This is the complete list. Nothing is hidden in small print.

Kharcha Check asks for

  • InternetOnly to download the public mutual fund price file from AMFI. It fetches the whole list, so nobody learns which funds you hold.
  • Notifications (optional)Optional. Bill reminders and daily nudges. Lock-screen alerts never show amounts.
  • Fingerprint or screen lockTo lock the app and unlock hidden entries.

It never asks for

  • SMS and call logPaste or share a bank message yourself if you want. Only that text is read.
  • MicrophoneThe 🎤 buttons use your phone’s own voice typing screen. The app gets only the words, never audio.
  • Contacts, location or advertising ID
  • Your whole gallery or storageFiles come through Android’s picker, one at a time, only when you choose them.
  • An account, phone number or email

Private by design, not by promise

A privacy policy is only words. These are the things the app actually does, so you don’t have to take anyone’s word for it.

This website follows the same rules

No cookies, no analytics, no ad trackers, and no fonts or scripts from other companies. Everything on this page loads from this site only. The source is on GitHub.

  • Encrypted databaseYour entries sit in an AES-256 encrypted database (SQLCipher). The key is held by Android Keystore, so it never leaves your phone’s secure hardware.
  • Kept out of cloud backupAndroid’s automatic cloud backup is switched off for the app. Your data goes to the cloud only if you set up a backup yourself.
  • Backups locked with your passwordBackup files are encrypted with AES-256-GCM before they’re written. Daily automatic backup can go to a folder you choose, like Google Drive.
  • Hidden entries and masked amountsHide sensitive entries behind your fingerprint, mask big numbers on Home, and block screenshots of the app if you like.
  • No SMS, everThe app has no SMS permission. If you paste or share a message, only that text is read, and account and reference numbers are masked.
  • Crash reports only if you say soIf the app crashes, the details are saved on the phone. Next time it asks whether you want to email them. Nothing is sent on its own.

Questions people ask first

Does Kharcha Check read my SMS?

No. The app doesn’t have SMS permission and can’t read your messages. If you want to add a spend from a bank message, copy it and tap Paste on Home, or share it to the app. Only the text you hand over is read.

Can I just say what I spent?

Yes. Tap 🎤 Speak on Home, or the mic in any name or note box, and say something like “450 for lunch with team at Paradise yesterday”. The amount, category, payee, note and date are filled in for you to check. It uses your phone’s own voice typing, so Kharcha Check needs no microphone permission and never hears audio.

Do I need to sign up?

No. There is no account, no phone number and no email. Open the app and start adding.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Adding entries, reports, reading statements and screenshots all work offline. Internet is used only to refresh mutual fund prices from AMFI if you track investments.

Which bank statements can it read?

PDF statements (including password-protected ones), scanned pages and photos, Excel and CSV downloads. HDFC, SBI, ICICI and credit card statements are covered by tests. If your bank’s layout isn’t read well, the import screen can send a reading report with names and account numbers masked.

What if I change or lose my phone?

Turn on automatic backup and choose a folder, for example in Google Drive. On the new phone, restore the backup with your password. Without a backup your data can’t be recovered, because no copy exists anywhere else. That’s the cost of real privacy.

How do I delete my data?

Go to Settings and choose Delete everything, or uninstall the app. There is no server copy to delete.

Is this financial advice?

No. The tips, projections and plans in the app are educational. Returns shown are examples, not promises. For big decisions, talk to a SEBI-registered adviser.
